## In Depth

`Numbers.ToRoman(number)`

Convert the input numbers into roman numerals. Only considers whole numbers (integers) (Eg. 1 becomes I). Made possible with Humanizer (https://github.com/Humanizr/Humanizer)

The inputs are:

- `number` (_integer_) — _Not documented yet._

Returns `result` (_string_).

Found in the library under **Actions**.
