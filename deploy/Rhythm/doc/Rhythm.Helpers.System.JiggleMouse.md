## In Depth

`System.JiggleMouse(runIt: false, interval: 0)`

This will move your mouse back and forth slowly while toggled true.

The inputs are:

- `runIt` (_boolean_, defaults to `false`) — True will cause the mouse to move on it's own.
- `interval` (_number_, defaults to `0`) — Time between movements (in seconds).

Returns nothing.

Found in the library under **Create**.
