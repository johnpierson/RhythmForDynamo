## In Depth

`Numbers.ToHeading(number, fullHeading: false)`

Convert the input numbers into headings: N,S,E,W or north, east, south or west. Made possible with Humanizer (https://github.com/Humanizr/Humanizer)

The inputs are:

- `number` (_number_) — _Not documented yet._
- `fullHeading` (_boolean_, defaults to `false`) — _Not documented yet._

Returns `result` (_string_).
